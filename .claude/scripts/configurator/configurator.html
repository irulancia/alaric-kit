Color

Auto: a near-white with a touch of Secondary, recalculated when Secondary changes. Untick it to pick your own colour. Either way, surfaces, borders and grey text follow the page, just as faint.
Button text contrastAPCA (the default) reads closer to what the eye sees on mid-tones (orange, mid blue), but it is a draft and can fall below 4.5:1. WCAG 2 AA (4.5:1) is the legal standard: choose it when compliance is required.

StyleThe same geometry in all three (Phosphor Icons, MIT). Size follows density: 16 / 20 / 24px on Normal.
Outline: buttons and pills become 2px outlines in their own colour. Duotone: the same outline plus a tinted fill. Filled keeps solid buttons.
One file for src/app.css: the font import, the :root tokens and every component style. Chrome and Edge open a save dialog and write the file; other browsers download it. “Copy tokens only” puts just the :root block on the clipboard.
